## User objective and acceptance boundary

Build server-friendly Crossref verification for new/modified BibTeX entries,
an automatically invalidated review cache, a one-time all-entry baseline, and
free automated fallbacks to reduce manual review. Final fallbacks are actual
PDF research using an LLM and then explicit human adjudication. Update the
README as features are integrated.

The user places exceptionally high importance on accuracy. Do not promise
100% accuracy or claim metadata agreement establishes truth. Preserve titles,
subtitles, accents, ordered authors, dates, publication types and editions.
Do not silently replace a preprint with a final publication, equate a book with
its review, or loosen matching just to clear the queue. Model confidence and
the existence of quoted text never grant approval.
