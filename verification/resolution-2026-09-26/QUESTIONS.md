## batch 02
1. Supplement series: own journal name ('... Supplement') or parent journal with the supplement in Number?
2. Monographs issued as numbered series: Pages 1--N rather than a page count? Volume from the bound-volume title page when the issue prints only a number?
3. Crossref chapter pagination (digitised book) vs author/institutional records for the print book, publisher page unreachable: which wins? (AllpEtal94: kept HEAD 421--452 vs Crossref 411-442)
4. Single form for supplement issues in Number ('4 Suppl 2', 'Supplement 1', 'Suppl 10')?
5. Titles with em dashes: '---' with spacing exactly as printed?

## batch 03
1. PubMed vs Crossref disagree only on last page, printed PDF unreachable: which wins? (KojiGold82 kept PubMed 43--49)
2. Items inside a journal's collective 'Abstracts' record (one DOI for pp 409-490, text unreadable): is the collective record enough to keep an entry the user marked as a real article, or confirm item by item?
3. Numbered monograph supplements catalogued as books (e.g. BJP Monograph Supplements no. 29, '94 p.'): pages = catalogue extent, text through bibliography, or @book with series/number and no pages?
4. Every official record prints a byline without a middle initial the cited entry has (Walter Pitts vs W H Pitts): does 'as printed' override 'never drop an initial only because a source omits it'?
