In a combined biochemical and histochemical investigation the following quantitative data o n a
unilateral nigro-neostriatal dopamine neuron system in the rat have been obtained or calculated.
- The number of neurons is 3,500 on the average. - There are about 130 pg dopamine per neuron
( I pg = lo-'* 9). - The terminals contain 50 (or 150) times more dopamine than the cell bodies.
-The terminal system of one neuron varies mainly between 55 and 77 cm in length and contains
about 500,000 varicosities. - The neostriatum, with a volume of 33 mm*, contains loBdopamine
varicosities occupying 0.3 yo of the volume, and 4,000,000 nerve cell bodies (not containing dopamine). - One average dopamine varicosity, with a diameter of 0.4 p , contains 2.5 x lo-' pg
dopamine, and has a doparnine concentration around 8,000 pg/g. - O n e dopamine cell body
of the substantia nigra, with a mean diameter of 30 p , contains 2.5 (or 0.8) pg dopamine, and has a
dopamine concentration around 200 (or 60) pg/g.

By means of the histochemical fluorescence method for the cellular localization of'
certain monoamines (Falck et al. 1962, Falck 1962, Corrodi and Hillarp 1963, 1964)
it has been shown that monoamines exist both in the cell bodies and in the nerve
terminals of special central neurons (Carlsson, Falck and Hillarp 1962, Dahlstrom
and Fuxe 1964, Fuxe 1965). In lesion experiments it has been possible to demonstrate
a number of central monoamine neuron systems (see AndCn et al. 1966). One of the
best known is a dopamine (DA) neuron system with the cell bodies in the substantia
nigra and the nerve terminals in the neostriatum (the caudate nucleus - putamen)
(AndCn et al. 1964, Bertler et al. 1964, And& et al. 1965 a, b). Since this nigroneostriatal DA neuron system is large and uncrossed and has well defined locations
of its cell bodies and nerve terminals, a quantitative calculation of the amine contents
in the different parts of this neuron system has been performed in the present investigation.
