## Cache hardening

Known DOI-linked PubMed and JATS corrections and retractions are now indexed separately
from entry fingerprints. A substantive edit, simultaneous key rename, or clean
baseline restore cannot erase them and permit a fresh positive Crossref result
to mask the warning. Only negative evidence transfers; previous positive field
judgments do not. The optional source-notices section in schema-2 snapshots
preserves these warnings even when no entry fingerprint matches. Legacy snapshots
also contribute their raw notice evidence before matching entries are restored.

The initial history migration indexed 74 distinct DOI-linked notice records in
2.29 seconds. It changed no additional current statuses. Incremental checkpoints
avoid scanning the entire audit history on every run. Source-notice adjudication
is still required for ChanEtal12b and MenoEtal96.
